# Digne Suits & More — editorial website prototype

Open `index.html` in your browser. Image assets are self-contained in `assets/`.

## Brand assets
The logo in `assets/logo.webp` is an AI-assisted reconstruction of the logo shown in the supplied business photo, **not** a verified master logo file. Nine accompanying store images were AI-enhanced from supplied Google Maps screenshots. They remove visible UI overlays, but may change details of garments, signage or physical interiors; validate with Digne before portraying them as exact inventory or store documentation.

## Working interactions
Responsive mobile navigation, collection filters, wishlist saved in browser storage, product preview, and an enquiry composer that copies a message. Booking is not confirmed and no backend/payment processing exists. Prices and availability must be supplied by the company. Phone number extracted from supplied promotional photograph: 0535046945.
